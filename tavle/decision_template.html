Can one trading decision run itself?

Every hour, Denmark's balancing market settles who was short and who was long. The direction repeats from one hour to the next far more often than not. This page builds a machine around that one fact: a registered rule, a learned gate, guards that refuse, a replay of fourteen months it never saw, and a set of faults you can switch on. Before the machine · five things to know

Denmark has two price zones, DK1 (Jutland and Funen) and DK2 (Zealand). Each hour gets a day-ahead price from an auction the day before. When the system then turns out short of power, Energinet activates reserves and the imbalance price ends above the day-ahead price; when it has too much, below. This page calls the difference the gap, in euros per megawatt-hour. When nothing had to be regulated the gap is zero.

The gap for an hour is settled and published shortly after the hour. A position for hour t must be placed in the intraday market by the gate, one hour before delivery, so the freshest settled hour a machine can see is t-2.

The rule is the one registered in the study this page grew from: take the sign of the last settled gap, one unit, for a cost of 0.6 euros per hour. The learned gate is a small neural network that gives the probability that the rule's direction is right. The guards are checks that refuse an hour: no settled data, no direction in it, a late feed, the wrong settlement regime.

Authority is earned, not granted. Every choice in the machine, which guards are on, how late the feed may be, what the gate is allowed to do, was measured on the even weeks of November 2021 to December 2023 and frozen. The odd weeks fitted the gate. January 2024 to 4 March 2025 was then replayed once and is called the separate period.

Nothing here is a strategy. A deliberate imbalance is prohibited, so the legitimate position is an intraday trade, and no intraday prices are public. Every euro figure is an upper bound settled at the imbalance price, as in the study.

Limits

Every euro figure is an upper bound settled at the imbalance price; an intraday position would only approach it, and a deliberate imbalance is prohibited. The signal is the settled gap, published after the hour; a live desk would use the activation direction as it happens, which this page does not load. The feed's lateness and the outage are injected, not observed. The imbalance series is hourly and ends 4 March 2025; the 15-minute successor is not loaded. One unit, no sizing: the gate's probability is worth more as a size than as a vote, and that is the next thing to measure, not measured here. Two zones, one country, one author who chose the questions.

Seven terms

gap imbalance price minus day-ahead price for an hour; positive when the system was short, zero when nothing was regulated
gate the moment a position for hour t must be placed, one hour before delivery; the freshest settled hour is t-2
rule take the sign of the last settled gap, one unit, at a cost of 0.6 euros per hour
learned gate a small network that gives the probability the rule's direction is right for this hour
guard a check that refuses an hour before any position is taken
authority what the learned gate is allowed to change, granted only where the even weeks showed it helps
separate period January 2024 to 4 March 2025, replayed once after every choice was frozen
