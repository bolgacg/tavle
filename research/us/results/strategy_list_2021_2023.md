# Strategies on the training years, 2021 to 2023

Written 6 Oct 2026 for my review, before any freeze. Training years only: 2020 is used for training, 2021 to 2023 are scored, and nothing from 1 January 2024 on was read. All numbers are in `strategy_list_2021_2023.json`; the table below is generated by `model/evaluate.py` (`strategy_list_2021_2023_table.md`).

## How every strategy was scored

- **Walk-forward.** Each month is predicted by a model refit on the 1st. It trains on delivery dates up to two days before the month, using only prices public by the month's first 05:00 deadline, expanding from January 2020. Idea B starts on 1 May 2021, five weeks into its weather data.
- **No choice uses the year it scores.**
  - Year Y uses the configuration, spike threshold and cut, C's five pairs, storm cut and zone set that did best on the year before. For the model-based ideas, that means one fit trained up to two days before Y-1, scored on Y-1, the same procedure as the rehearsal. So 2023 reuses the v2 rehearsal's choices and predictions.
  - 2021 has no earlier year. It uses defaults declared before any result: the first regression configuration, and spike S = 50 with p* = 0.05. C's 2021 pairs come from a 2020 walk-forward, and the storm cut is chosen on 2020.
  - Deep strategies are the exception, marked with an asterisk.
- **Costs** are the audited full costs. Every cleared MWh pays Rate Schedule 1 plus the FERC charge, and supply also pays the uplift bound. The 0.50 column replaces all of it with 0.50 USD/MWh.
- **The proposed bar** has four conditions: total net above zero after full costs; positive in at least 2 of the 3 years; annualised Sharpe above 0.42 (S&P 500 long run, from daily net P&L with zero days included); and total still positive at the 0.50 cost.

## How to read the list

- **Nine strategies pass the bar,** but most of the money comes from one week in 2022.
  - On 23 and 24 December 2022 (Winter Storm Elliott), always-supply lost 148,090 USD in a single day, and strategies that held virtual load gained.
  - The storm-day flip's whole 2022 profit (282,065) sits on its five best days. Over the three years it keeps only +24,260 without them.
  - B with gradient boosting keeps +329,676 without its five best days. It is the only leader that stays large.
  - The column "Total without its 5 best days" shows this concentration for every strategy.
- **2023, the most recent year, turns against the two-sided ideas.** B and D (both models), the storm flip, A spike with the deep model, and the baseline lost money in 2023.
  - Every strategy that made money in 2023 sells supply or trades pairs: always-supply +68,154; the tail flag +49,792; A as first registered +39,058; C deep +34,092; zone subset +27,324; the deep rolling cut +22,117; risk-sized supply +21,425; A spike flag +20,542; storm-day filter +15,185; A as first registered with the deep model +12,745; C +3,911; A hourly mean +2,638.
  - These are development numbers. Only the held-out years can say which pattern lasts.
- **No sign of lookahead in the leader.** B's 2022 forecasts correlate 0.12 with the hourly gap (0.09 without the most extreme 1% of hours). That matches its 2022 validation. Its features passed the real-data test in which everything published after 05:00 is replaced by noise.
- **Number of tries: 19 strategies are in this list.**
  - Inside them, each choice compared several configurations: 8 per regression; 6 spike threshold and cut pairs; 2 cuts for the tail flag; 4 storm cuts each for filter and flip; 6 rolling cuts; and 55 zone pairs, from which C takes five.
  - The v1 and v2 rehearsals scored 2023 alone, with the same models.
  - With this many tries, the best total is partly luck. Weigh the leader against the 19 tries, and treat the intervals as unadjusted.
- **Choices per year** (configurations, S and p*, pairs, cuts, zones) are in the JSON under "choices".
  - The A spike flag used S = 50 in 2021 (default), then S = 25 chosen on 2021 and on 2022.
  - The tail flag (idea 9) used p* = 0.05, 0.05 and 0.02.
  - Its walk-forward probabilities for the strategy lab are in `idea9_wf_2021_2023.parquet` (gene and here), with the per-year cuts in the .json.
